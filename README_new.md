## 📖 Description
**Fabled Roots** is an RPG-lite data pack that adds **10 unique races** and **10 diverse classes**, along with new weapons, items, spells and structures. When you first join a world or server, you choose a race and a class to shape your playstyle. Changed your mind? You can reset your choices later from the pause menu. Everything is designed to enhance the classic Minecraft gameplay loop, giving your adventure a fresh twist without getting in the way.

<br>

## 🔎 Content Overview (Wiki)
Want to know what each race can do, which class fits you best or where to find the new weapons? It's all in our **[Wiki](https://wiki.explorerseden.eu/en/fabled_roots/home)**:
- 🧙‍♂️ **Races & Classes**: perks, drawbacks, home turfs and abilities of all 10 races, plus the perks of all 10 classes
- 🗡️ **Weapons & Items**: race weapons, armor and other new gear
- 📜 **Spells**: how to craft and enchant Spell Scrolls and what every spell does
- 🏚️ **Structures & Descendants**: race Shelters and Outposts, their NPCs and the journals hidden inside

<br>

## 🛠️ Technical Gibberish
Admins/OPs can configure several aspects in game with `/function fabled_roots`.

This data pack also works as the **required** resource pack. Just put the same file into your `resourcepacks` folder and enable it in game so the textures show up properly.

_(Not needed for the auto-converted mod versions in Single-Player.)_

**PaperMC users:** set the `paper.maxChatCommandInputSize` property to a value between 512 and 1024 (see the [PaperMC Docs](https://docs.papermc.io/paper/reference/system-properties/#papermaxchatcommandinputsize)) and enable `allow-non-player-entities-on-scoreboards` in your Paper config.
